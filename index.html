Factor register & methodology
• Scope 1 fuel combustion: IPCC 2006 default factors converted to approximate per-unit factors from published factor tables: diesel 2.66085 kg CO₂/litre; petrol 2.29886 kg CO₂/litre; LPG 1.58247 kg CO₂/kg. These are generic combustion CO₂ estimates, not India-specific and not full lifecycle CO₂e. Replace with supplier-specific fuel data if available.
• Scope 2: Central Electricity Authority (CEA), CO₂ Baseline Database v22.0, FY 2025–26 weighted-average grid factor 0.675 t CO₂/MWh = 0.675 kg CO₂/kWh. Use the factor corresponding to the reporting year; this is a location-based grid estimate.
• Scope 3 biological waste treatment: NITI Aayog 2026 Waste sector report reproduces IPCC 2006 defaults. Composting: CH₄ 4 kg/t wet or 10 kg/t dry; N₂O 0.24 kg/t wet or 0.6 kg/t dry. Anaerobic digestion: CH₄ 0.8 kg/t wet or 2 kg/t dry; N₂O negligible. Converted with AR6 GWP100 CH₄ biogenic = 27 and N₂O = 273; resulting proxies are 173.52 kg CO₂e/t composting wet, 433.8 dry, 21.6 wet AD, 54 dry AD. Defaults, not measured Guwahati factors; exclude captured methane/plant-specific performance unless recorded separately.
• Scope 3 road transport: India GHG Program 2015 stakeholder-consultation estimates: LDV 0.3070, MDV 0.5928, HDV 0.7375 kg CO₂/km. Historic estimates; CO₂ only, not CO₂e, no payload/load adjustment. Use only as a disclosed demo proxy; prefer measured fuel/activity and an approved current factor set.
• Landfill: no one-size-fits-all per-tonne factor is applied. Methane depends on waste composition, degradable organic carbon, landfill conditions, methane recovery and time. Entry is saved as pending/unquantified until a site-specific or approved model factor is provided. Recycling/MRF is not given a negative “avoided emissions” credit by default.
This prototype inventory is not an assurance-ready GHG statement. It excludes refrigerant leakage, wastewater, purchased goods/capital goods, employee commuting, business travel, upstream fuel/electricity emissions and other relevant categories unless separately added. Confirm organisational boundary, reporting year, operational control, source records and factor policy before formal reporting.
