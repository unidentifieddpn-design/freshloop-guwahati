2 · Waste category

Four-stream model
Regulatory design basis: India’s Solid Waste Management Rules, 2026 establish four-stream segregation (wet, dry, sanitary and special care). This prototype is a workflow aid, not a legal determination. Follow current CPCB/SPCB directions and GMC contract / authorised-facility requirements.

Carbon estimate

DEFRA 2026 basis
DEFRA / DESNZ published 2026 greenhouse-gas conversion factors on 11 June 2026 and updated the flat file in July. The factors are primarily designed for UK reporting; use in India should be disclosed as a proxy. Official factor files ↗
Enter the exact factor for your vehicle class from the official 2026 flat file. It is intentionally blank until verified; the app will not invent a carbon result.
